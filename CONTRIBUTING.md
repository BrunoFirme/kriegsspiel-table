# Contributing

Fixes, ideas and pieces of work are welcome. Before a change goes in:

- `powershell -File tools\check.ps1` passes: both builds, every test, the app's node tests, and the size and
  hygiene rules. [`docs/architecture.md`](docs/architecture.md) explains how the code is put together.
- The change is your own work, or you say where it comes from and under what license. No code, pictures, models
  or scripts from other people's modules or games without their permission.
