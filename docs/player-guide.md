# The player's guide

The table is yours as much as the umpire's: keep your own map, study a scenario, make pieces, watch a game, or
run one yourself.

## Your own map

- **Scenarios** → **New blank scenario…**, or **Open** on any scenario you have: that table is yours alone. Lay your
  map on it, place your blocks as the reports come in and move them as your orders go out. Nobody joins it unless you
  invite them.
- Any installed scenario or pack opens the same way, to study the ground and the pieces.
- The **ruler** (M), **ranging** and **drawing** work here as at any table; see the
  [umpire's guide](umpire-guide.md#at-the-table).
- **Ctrl+Shift+C** copies a region to the clipboard, to paste into your messages.

## Watching a game

The host can let you in as an **observer**:

1. **Join a game**, then type the address, port, password and your name.
2. Wait at the door until the host or a lead lets you in; ✕ cancels. If they refuse or the host does not answer,
   you are back on the title screen with the reason. Next time you come straight back in, even at a new address.

An observer sees the whole board and changes nothing; the umpires' zones, pointers and dice-panel rolls stay with
them.

## Running a game

Anyone can host. The [umpire's guide](umpire-guide.md) covers hosting, the door and every tool.

## Your own pieces

**Piece studio → New pack…** draws blocks or turns pictures into pieces: see
[the piece studio](community-guide.md#the-piece-studio).
