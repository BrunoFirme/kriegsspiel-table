## First steps

**Bring your pieces in.** However your game reaches you, it ends up as packs of pieces and scenarios on this PC.

- **A digital tabletop module:** **Import** in the title screen's menu. Pick it from the list, **Browse…** to its
  `.json`, or paste its Steam Workshop link. You get a pack with every piece and a scenario laid out as in the
  module; [Convert a module](community-guide.md#convert-a-digital-tabletop-module) has the details.
- **A `.ktpack` file someone shared:** drop it on the window, or **Import** → **Browse…** to it. It holds a pack, a
  scenario, or both.
- **Pictures or models of your own:** **Piece studio → New pack…**, then **＋ New piece** turns pictures, 3D models
  or a PDF into pieces, or makes blocks, tokens and figures. Pictures dropped on the window become pieces too.
- **Someone else's game:** **Join a game** with the address and password they send. Their map and pieces come to
  you with the table; there is nothing to install.

**Set up a table.**

1. **Scenarios → Open** on the scenario an import brought, or **New blank scenario…** for an empty table
   (**Settings → New scenarios** sets the backdrop and surface it starts with).
2. **Map** drawer: add map sheets (pictures or web links), drag each into place and lock it; **Map scale…** makes
   the ruler read real distances.
3. **Pieces** panel: every pack on this PC is listed. Drag a piece onto the table (Shift keeps placing); placing from
   a pack brings it into the game for everyone at the table. The table saves itself.
4. **Q / E** turn, **= / −** size, **[ ]** step, the wheel zooms; right-click a piece or the table for the rest.
5. **Settings → Show the tour again** walks through the screens once more.

**Finding things.** The box at the top left searches all of help. The box at the top right finds words on this
page. Over a tool on the table, **F1** opens its section.
